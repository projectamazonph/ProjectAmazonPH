## 3. Brand Voice

### Voice Characteristics
| Trait | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | No hedging, no "maybe" | "You will land your first client in 60 days." |
| **Encouraging** | Believes in the reader | "You're closer than you think." |
| **Specific** | Numbers, timelines, proof | "₱50M+ in managed ad spend." |
| **Bilingual** | Taglish where natural | "Hindi natatakot sa real campaigns." |
| **Anti-Fluff** | Zero filler | Cut every word that doesn't earn its place. |

### Voice Rules
1. **Lead with the outcome, not the feature.** "Land ₱80k/month clients" not "Learn PPC fundamentals."
2. **Use second person.** "You" not "students" or "VAs."
3. **Concrete over abstract.** "₱60k/month" not "high income."
4. **Social proof in every section.** Names, numbers, timelines.
5. **Urgency without manipulation.** Real scarcity (5 spots) not fake countdowns.

### Taglines & Hooks
- "From Zero to ₱80k+/Month"
- "The PPC specialist na hindi natatakot sa real campaigns"
- "Clients actively hunt for you — and pay premium rates"
- "I was a general VA, just like you. I know the journey."
- "No fluff. Just the strategies that get results."

## 7. Brand Guidelines Summary

### Do
- Lead with outcomes and numbers
- Use real student names and results
- Write in Taglish where it feels natural
- Show the journey (VA → specialist → ₱80k+)
- Use urgency based on real scarcity

### Don't
- Use generic "high income" without specifics
- Promise results without proof
- Write in pure academic English
- Hide pricing (transparency builds trust)
- Use stock photos of non-Filipino people

### Always Include
- Social proof (testimonials, numbers, timelines)
- Clear CTA (enroll, learn more, download)
- Value stack (bonuses, what's included)
- Urgency (spots available, enrollment closing)
