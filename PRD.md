**Version:** 2.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-09

## Product Overview

ProjectAmazonPH is an **Amazon PPC coaching and training ecosystem** purpose-built for Filipino virtual assistants. Founded by Ryan Roland Dabao (14-year VA veteran, 8-year PPC specialist, ₱50M+ in managed ad spend), the ecosystem transforms Filipino VAs from general virtual assistants earning ₱15k/month into specialized Amazon PPC experts earning ₱80k+/month.

**Core Value Proposition:** "From Zero to ₱80k+/Month" — structured PPC coaching that bridges the VA-to-specialist salary gap through proven systems, hands-on training, and gamified progression.

## Target Audience

### Primary: Filipino VAs
- Current VAs earning ₱15k–₱30k/month
- Wanting to specialize in high-value Amazon PPC
- Self-motivated, English-proficient, tech-literate
- Age: 22–35, typically with 1–5 years VA experience

### Secondary: Career Shifters
- BPO workers transitioning to VA industry
- Fresh graduates with business/marketing degrees
- Returning OFWs seeking remote work

## User Stories

### Prospective Student
- US-01: I can learn about the program through Facebook, YouTube, and the website
- US-02: I can see clear pricing, curriculum, and outcomes before purchasing
- US-03: I can choose a tier that fits my budget and commitment level
- US-04: I can join a community of fellow students for support

### Enrolled Student
- US-05: I can progress through structured PPC training at my own pace
- US-06: I can access exercises, templates, and real PPC scenarios
- US-07: I can get coaching feedback on my campaign builds
- US-08: I can connect with alumni for networking and job referrals
- US-09: I can track my progress and skill development

### Alumni / Graduate
- US-10: I can access ongoing resources and community support
- US-11: I can mentor new students (earn while learning)
- US-12: I can get job placement support through the network
