## Brand Voice Reminders
- Lead with outcomes, not features
- Use "you" not "students" or "VAs"
- Concrete numbers: ₱ amounts, timelines, percentages
- Taglish where natural
- Always end with a CTA (comment, save, share, link)
- Max 150 words for feed posts (shorter = better reach)
