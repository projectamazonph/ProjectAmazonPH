## 3. Channel Strategy

### A. Content Marketing (Priority 1 — Free, Compounds)

**Blog** — Publish 2-4 posts/month on projectamazonph.com
- **Pillar content:** "What is Amazon PPC" guide, "How Much Do PPC VAs Earn" data post
- **Student stories:** Real interviews with Maria, James, Liza (get real details)
- **Tactical posts:** "How to Set Up Your First Amazon Campaign" (free value)
- **Behind-the-scenes:** Coach's daily routine, client results, industry insights

**YouTube** — Weekly 5-10 min videos
- "Amazon PPC Explained in 5 Minutes" (search volume play)
- "Day in the Life of a PPC Specialist" (aspirational content)
- "Live Campaign Audit" (proof of expertise)
- Student interviews (social proof)

**TikTok/Reels** — 3-5 short clips/week
- "₱15k to ₱80k in 8 Weeks" story clips
- Quick PPC tips (30-60 seconds)
- Student win celebrations
- "Things I Wish I Knew Before Starting PPC"

### B. SEO (Priority 1 — Free, Compounds)

**Target Keywords:**
| Keyword | Volume | Difficulty | Intent |
|---------|--------|------------|--------|
| amazon ppc training philippines | Low | Low | Transactional |
| how to become amazon ppc specialist | Medium | Medium | Informational |
| amazon advertising virtual assistant | Medium | Medium | Transactional |
| pva amazon salary philippines | Low | Low | Informational |
| amazon ppc for beginners | High | High | Informational |
| va training amazon philippines | Low | Low | Transactional |
| how much do amazon pva earn | Low | Low | Informational |
| amazon ppc specialist salary | Medium | Medium | Informational |
| amazon fba advertising course | Medium | Medium | Transactional |
| virtual assistant amazon specialist | Medium | Medium | Transactional |

**On-Page SEO:**
- Title tags: Primary keyword + brand
- Meta descriptions: Benefit-driven, include ₱ amount
- H1/H2 structure: Keyword-rich, natural
- Internal linking: Blog → Landing page, Blog → Products
- Image alt text: Descriptive, keyword-inclusive

**Technical SEO:**
- Custom domain (projectamazonph.com)
- Mobile-first design (already responsive)
- Page speed optimization (Netlify CDN helps)
- Schema markup (Course, Product, Review)
- XML sitemap + robots.txt

### D. Email Marketing (Priority 2 — Nurture)

**Lead Magnets** (free value to capture emails):
1. "Amazon PPC Glossary for Filipino VAs" — 50 terms explained
2. "₱80k PPC Specialist Income Calculator" — show the math
3. "First 30 Days as a PPC Specialist" — roadmap PDF
4. "PPC Specialist vs General VA: Income Comparison" — data sheet

**Email Sequences:**
1. **Welcome (5 emails):** Introduce Coach, show results, present offer
2. **Nurture (weekly):** PPC tips, student stories, industry news
3. **Sales (launch):** Enrollment open, urgency, testimonials
4. **Post-purchase:** Onboarding, community invite, upsell
