## 7. Optimization & Trust Signals Cheat Sheet

| Element | Standard Phrase (Avoid) | Conversion Phrase (Use) | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authenticity** | "Quality products" | "100% Authenticity Guaranteed" | Specificity kills doubt. |
| **Delivery** | "Estimated delivery" | "Guaranteed Delivery by [Date]" | Predictability is a premium. |
| **Payments** | "Pay via e-wallet" | "One-tap payment via GCash/Maya" | Emphasizes ease of use. |
| **Trust** | "Customer satisfaction" | "Zero-Budol Guarantee" | Uses local slang to address a specific pain point. |
| **CTA** | "Click here" | "Shop Authentic Now" | Ties the action to the core value prop. |
| **Social Proof** | "Many people love us" | "The Professional's Choice in Metro Manila" | Creates an aspirational identity. |
